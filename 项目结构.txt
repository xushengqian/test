═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
                        推荐使用方案
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

场景1: 简单上传，无特殊要求
推荐: FileUploadUtils.java
代码: 
    FileUploadUtils.uploadBytes(url, bytes, name, field, null)

场景2: 需要显示进度
推荐: AdvancedExamples.ProgressUploader
代码:
    ProgressUploader.uploadWithProgress(url, bytes, name, field, callback)

场景3: 需要可靠性（自动重试）
推荐: AdvancedExamples.RetryUploader
代码:
    RetryUploader.uploadWithRetry(url, bytes, name, field, 3)

场景4: 大文件上传
推荐: AdvancedExamples.ChunkedUploader
代码:
    ChunkedUploader.uploadLargeFile(url, bytes, name, field, chunkSize)

场景5: 批量上传
推荐: FileUploadUtils.uploadMultipleFiles
代码:
    uploadMultipleFiles(url, fileList, field)

场景6: 异步上传（不阻塞）
推荐: HttpClientFileUploadExample.uploadFileAsync
代码:
    uploadFileAsync(url, bytes, name, field)
